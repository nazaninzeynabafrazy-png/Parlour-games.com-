The Parlour — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This policy explains what The Parlour — Game Table ("the game") does with information. The game is made by [YOUR NAME OR STUDIO]. Contact: Parlour.games@gmail.com.

1. No accounts, no sign-up

You do not need to create an account, and the game does not ask for your name, email, phone number or location.

2. What is stored on your device

Your scores, badges, settings (language, colours, music choice), game progress and gem balance are saved only on your own device, in the browser's local storage. We do not receive them. Clearing your browser or app data erases them.

If you choose "Play my own music…", the audio file you pick is played on your device only and is not uploaded anywhere.

3. Advertising

The game is free and is supported by advertising. It may show banner ads, full-screen ads between games, and optional "watch an ad" videos that earn gems.

  • Ads may come from a sponsor we deal with directly or from an advertising network.
  • If an advertising network is used, that network may collect device information such as your advertising ID, IP address and approximate location, to show and measure ads. It acts under its own privacy policy, which we will link here: [AD NETWORK NAME AND PRIVACY LINK].
  • You can reset or turn off personalised ads in your device settings (on Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Until a network or sponsor is connected, the game shows only a plain placeholder box that collects nothing.

4. Gems and invite codes

Gems are free in-game points earned by watching ads or inviting friends. They have no cash value and cannot be bought or sold. Invite codes are random codes; using one does not reveal your identity.

5. Online-player counter (optional)

If switched on, the game contacts a small counting service to show how many people are playing. It sends a random anonymous identifier made on your device and the invite code, if any. It does not send your name, contacts or scores. If it is off, the game estimates busy times from the clock on your device.

5b. Offline copy

The website saves a copy of the game in your browser (a "service worker" cache) so it can work without internet. It contains only the game files, nothing about you. Clearing site data removes it.

6. Fonts, libraries and hosting

The offline version loads nothing from other websites for its fonts and graphics. The website version is hosted by [HOSTING PROVIDER, e.g. Cloudflare Pages], which may keep ordinary server logs (such as IP address) for security.

7. Children

The game is suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. [If the app is listed as child-directed, ads must use child-safe settings — check your store's rules.]

8. Your choices

You can delete everything the game has saved by clearing its data in your browser or app settings. For any question or request, write to Parlour.games@gmail.com.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
